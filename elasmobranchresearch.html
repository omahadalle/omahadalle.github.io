Research

Sharks and rays of Somalia

Documenting the importance of sharks and rays to Somalia's fisheries, and the conservation gaps that leave them unprotected.

Background

In Somalia, domestic fishers and commercial fisheries have targeted coastal sharks for decades. This research initiative documents how important sharks are to the fishery, and explores the challenges and possible solutions for key conservation issues.

Fieldwork

My fieldwork has taken me to landing sites across the Bari region and to the Mogadishu fish market. Through photography and data collection, I document catch composition and the realities of the domestic shark trade.

Contributions

  • IUCN SSC Shark Specialist Group Regular contributor since 2022, supporting species assessments for sharks, rays and chimaeras in Somalia.
  • Important Shark and Ray Areas (ISRA) Project Supporting the project's work to identify critical habitat for sharks and rays.
  • The Global Status of Sharks, Rays, and Chimaeras Co-author of the Somalia section, documenting the state of research, fisheries and conservation gaps across the country.
